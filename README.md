# HallPals

Residence life, built around the people who live and work in it.

**[Visit hallpals.com](https://www.hallpals.com) · [Download on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/hallpals/id6756520732)**

After three years as a Resident Assistant, I kept running into the same small frustrations: scattered tools, repetitive reporting, and information that was harder to find than it should be. I started HallPals as a personal project to make my own day-to-day work easier and give me more time to focus on residents.

It grew into an app shaped by the actual RA workflow. Other RAs began testing it and sharing feedback, and I later demoed it to ResLife leadership, who responded enthusiastically. HallPals is now available on the App Store.

The idea is simple: bring the everyday pieces of residence life together so residents, RAs, and staff can stay connected and informed.

- **For residents:** find on-duty staff, send messages, report concerns, and reach campus resources.
- **For RAs and staff:** track duty rounds, log incidents, manage duty status, and coordinate communication.

Features vary by user role and university configuration.
